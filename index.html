About Me

I am a PhD candidate researching black hole jets, astrochemistry, relativistic hydrodynamics, and jet–ISM interactions. My work combines ALMA, IRAM 30m, MeerKAT and ATCA observations with numerical simulations to understand how relativistic outflows shape the interstellar medium.

Research

Astrochemistry & Jet Calorimetry

Using molecular gas as a calorimeter to constrain the energetics of black hole jets and quantify jet feedback.

Relativistic Hydrodynamic Simulations

Developing relativistic hydrodynamic simulations to reproduce ejecta interacting with realistic molecular environments.

Supermassive Black Holes & Galaxy Evolution

Observational studies of AGN feedback and the connection between black holes and their host galaxies.

Exoplanets

Transit and radial-velocity analyses contributing to exoplanet characterization projects.
